# Mediversal-Hospital

# 📊 MGH KPI Report: Data-Driven Insights for Strategic Decision-Making

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is a **high-level KPI report** for **Massachusetts General Hospital (MGH)**, designed to help the executive team quickly understand key performance metrics and uncover insights into **admissions, readmissions, patient costs, insurance coverage, and hospital efficiency**. 

The analysis is based on **synthetic patient data (2011–2022)** covering demographics, insurance, medical encounters, and procedures. The goal is to **optimize decision-making**, improve hospital operations, and ensure financial sustainability.

---
## 🏥 Objectives
- **Track patient admissions and readmissions** over time.
- **Analyze hospital efficiency** through **Length of Stay (LOS) trends**.
- **Evaluate financial risks** by identifying high-cost procedures and gaps in **insurance coverage**.
- **Provide strategic recommendations** for improving patient care and operational efficiency.

---
## 📊 Dataset Summary
- **1,000+ synthetic patient records** (2011–2022)
- Key attributes analyzed:
  - **Patient Demographics** (Age, Gender, Marital Status, Race)
  - **Encounter Types** (Inpatient, Outpatient, Emergency, Ambulatory, Urgent Care, Wellness)
  - **Admissions & Readmissions** (Trends, LOS, Readmission rates)
  - **Insurance Coverage & Costs** (Cost burden by coverage category)
  - **Procedure Costs** (High-risk financial zones: Atrial Fibrillation, Pregnancy Procedures)

---
## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings
### 1️⃣ **Admissions & Readmissions Trends**
- **6-month rolling average of admissions is stable** at **180-205**.
- **Ambulatory encounters dominate (40%)** for patients aged **78-90 years**.
- **Readmission rates highlight inefficiencies**, requiring better follow-up care strategies.

### 2️⃣ **Hospital Efficiency (LOS Analysis)**
- **Inpatient stays average 36.9 hours**, with a slight increase from last year.
- **Emergency visits are highly efficient** (average **1.5 hours**).
- **Wellness & urgent care visits are optimized** (~0.2-0.3 hours).

### 3️⃣ **Financial Risks & Insurance Gaps**
- **49.78% of Atrial Fibrillation procedure costs are borne by uninsured patients** (~$22,000 per procedure).
- **60% of Common Pregnancy-related procedures lack full insurance coverage**, posing financial risks (~$3,500 per procedure).
- **Encouraging better insurance selection** could reduce **out-of-pocket costs** for high-risk procedures.

---
## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations
✅ **Reduce Readmission Rates:**
   - Implement **predictive modeling** for high-risk patients.
   - Enhance **post-discharge care coordination**.

✅ **Optimize Hospital Efficiency:**
   - Use **real-time data tracking** for LOS optimization.
   - Improve **bed utilization** and **triage efficiency**.

✅ **Mitigate Financial Risks:**
   - **Educate patients** about insurance coverage benefits.
   - **Optimize pricing structures** for uninsured/underinsured patients.
   - Strengthen **partnerships with insurers** to increase coverage.
